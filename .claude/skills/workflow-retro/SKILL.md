---
name: workflow-retro
description: Review a requested period of completed tasks and failures to propose evidence-based workflow improvements.
---

Read `docs/guides/retrospective.md` and use `docs/templates/retro.md`. Gather actual
closeouts and execution evidence within the requested period. Separate observed
causes from hypotheses. Historical stage reports are background, not current-period
activity. If evidence is insufficient, say so.

Propose focused changes with observable success criteria. Record unknown costs
and usage rather than inventing estimates. Produce a local report or chat response;
external messages, publication and scheduled recurrence need their own authorization.
Avoid adding a new universal rule for a single task-specific failure.
